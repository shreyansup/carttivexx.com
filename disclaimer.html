When to See a Doctor

If you are experiencing any of the following, consult a healthcare professional before using CartiVex:

  • Persistent, severe, or worsening joint pain
  • Swelling, redness, or warmth around joints
  • Loss of joint function or mobility
  • Joint pain following an injury or accident
  • Any diagnosed joint condition (e.g., rheumatoid arthritis, lupus, gout)

CartiVex is designed as a supportive nutritional habit for generally healthy adults who want to maintain joint comfort as part of an active lifestyle. It is not designed to replace medical evaluation or treatment.

Ingredient Safety & Interactions

While CartiVex is formulated with generally well-tolerated ingredients, you should consult a healthcare provider before use if you:

  • Are taking blood-thinning medications (e.g., warfarin, aspirin, clopidogrel) — turmeric and curcumin may interact with these
  • Are pregnant or nursing
  • Have gallbladder disease or gallstones — turmeric may stimulate bile production
  • Have diabetes — some ingredients may affect blood sugar levels
  • Are scheduled for surgery — discontinue use at least 2 weeks before any surgical procedure
  • Have iron deficiency — curcumin may interfere with iron absorption
  • Are taking any prescription medications or other supplements
